# Collaborative Writing

Read this file when multiple authors, reviewers, approvers, clients, editors, or organizations supply directions or feedback.

When multiple agent skills or qualified specialist roles also apply, first read [skill-composition.md](skill-composition.md) and map their subject and artifact authority separately from human stakeholder approval.

## Map authority

Record each stakeholder's role, decision authority, scope, supplied directions, and approval state. Distinguish:

- governing requirements that cannot be waived by an ordinary reviewer;
- author-owned meaning and voice decisions;
- subject-matter or evidence decisions;
- editorial recommendations;
- organizational style rules; and
- preferences that remain optional.

Do not treat seniority, repetition, or the latest comment as automatic authority. Use the documented direction hierarchy and surface unresolved conflicts.

An auditor, reviewer, or specialist owns findings only within their assigned scope; a finding does not confer authority to change the artifact. In Audit, keep the inspected version unchanged and route every proposed correction to the documented revision decision owner.

## Consolidate feedback

Normalize comments into stable issues with location, request, rationale, authority, affected requirement or evidence, conflicts, proposed response, owner, and disposition. Merge duplicates without erasing materially different reasons.

When reviewers disagree:

1. identify the exact conflict;
2. state which requirement, evidence, audience need, or authority supports each position;
3. distinguish compatible changes from mutually exclusive choices;
4. recommend only when the governing basis supports it; and
5. request the decision from the person who owns it.

## Protect voice and attribution

Do not flatten multiple contributors into generic institutional prose unless that is the approved target. Distinguish individual author voice, publication voice, quoted contributor language, and content ownership. Do not infer that a collaborator approved wording merely because their comment was addressed.

## Handoff

Report resolved, partially resolved, rejected, deferred, and blocked feedback; decisions still needed; affected artifact versions; requirements or claims requiring recheck; and the next approval owner. Distinguish the audited version from any separately revised version. Do not call the artifact approved until the authorized approver accepts the relevant version.

# Interview and Oral-History Evidence

Read this file for interviews, oral histories, witness accounts, subject responses, recorded conversations, questionnaires, or attributed personal testimony.

## Establish the evidence contract

Record participant identifier, interviewer, date, medium, consent basis, recording and transcription status, permitted attribution, quotation and paraphrase permissions, review or correction rights, embargo, retention, privacy, safety, and publication boundaries.

Use the participant's actual agreement. Do not infer consent, authority, anonymity, or permission from participation alone. Distinguish **On record**, **On background**, **Anonymous attribution**, **Off record**, and **Unresolved** according to the governing agreement; do not invent a universal meaning when the project defines these terms differently.

## Capture faithfully

- Preserve speaker identity and turn boundaries.
- Record whether text is verbatim transcript, corrected transcript, notes, memory, translation, or model-generated summary.
- Use timestamps or stable locators for material quotations.
- Mark inaudible, uncertain, interrupted, translated, or reconstructed wording.
- Separate interviewer framing from participant claims.
- Never clean grammar in a quotation without transparent permitted editing.

## Verify and reconcile

Treat testimony as evidence of what the participant reported, not automatic proof of the underlying event. Compare material claims with records and independent sources when appropriate. Preserve conflicting recollections and time-distance limits.

When fairness or publication stakes warrant it, maintain questions for the subject, fact-check queries, responses, response deadlines, refusals, corrections, and right-of-reply status. Do not imply endorsement because a participant reviewed only factual details.

## Protect participants

Minimize sensitive information, protect promised identity treatment, and avoid publishing details that re-identify an anonymous participant. Escalate risks involving minors, trauma, health, immigration, employment, retaliation, allegations, or legal exposure. Qualified ethical or legal review remains outside Unsloop.

## Return the boundary

State transcript type, inspected range, attribution status, permissions, corrections, corroboration, unresolved disputes, subject-response status, and restrictions that affect drafting or publication. In Audit, do not silently clean, replace, omit, or reattribute testimony; propose any manuscript or transcript change separately with its permission and semantic effect.
